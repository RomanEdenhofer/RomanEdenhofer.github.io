Quantum algorithms & complexity theory

Roman Edenhofer

I am a PhD student at IRIF in Paris, supervised by Simon Apers and Frédéric Magniez. I started my PhD in March 2024.

I am interested in quantum algorithms and complexity theory, with a particular focus on space complexity.

I also organize the Algorithms and Complexity Seminar at IRIF.
